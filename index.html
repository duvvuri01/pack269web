ABOUT OUR PACK

Small acts with big heart

Pack 269 builds character, citizenship, and confidence through outdoor adventure, hands-on STEM, and service. We primarily serve the Parkmont, Niles, and Vallejo Mill schools but welcome girls and boys in grades K-5 from all over the area. We meet on Wednesdays in the historic Veterans' Memorial Building in Niles.

OUTDOOR SKILLS

Hikes, campouts, nature study, and leave-no-trace basics.

STEM & CREATIVITY

From Pinewood Derby cars to hands-on projects, Scouts design, build, and discover new skills.

COMMUNITY SERVICE

Pack service days and projects that make a difference.

FUN & FRIENDS

Games, campfires, and celebrations that build our community.

Year at a Glance

We have a full slate of activities planned this year, with multiple overnight experiences, fun DIY activities, and plenty of time together in service. Our welcome packet has lots more information on what to expect and how to sign up.
