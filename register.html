4 EASY STEPS

How to join our pack.

Paperwork is the least fun part, but we've tried to make it easy.
Questions? Email pack269info@gmail.com

1

Register with Scouting America

Official youth registration for Scouting America, pays National + Council fees ($85 + $149). Optional subscription to Scout Life magazine ($15).

GO TO SCOUTING.ORG →

Select unit: Pack 269 Fremont • Mission Peak District • Golden Gate Area Council

2

Tell us about your scout.

This helps us track membership, parent contact info, volunteer interest, and payment for pack dues.

OPEN GOOGLE FORM →
3

Pay Pack Dues

Pack dues $115-140 cover badges, awards, supplies for activities, camping reservations. Leaders are 100% volunteers.

ZELLE PREFERRED
anup.pokhrel90@gmail.com
Include scout name in memo. $115 for grades K-1, $140 for grades 2-5.
If you cannot pay due to financial difficulty, ask a leader about scholarships.
4

Get Uniform & Book

Uniform: official shirt, neckerchief & slide for your rank (~$50). Handbook is useful, pants optional.
